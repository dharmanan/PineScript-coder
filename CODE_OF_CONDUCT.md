# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kohen Pine Studio to be a respectful, practical and welcoming place to discuss code, indicator design and responsible testing. Everyone participating in this project is expected to act in good faith and treat others with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific and respectful.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical or trading-related ideas.
- Keep feedback focused on code, documented behavior and reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy; never request or share credentials, private keys, account data or personal trading information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, scams, market manipulation or guaranteed-profit claims.
- Sharing credentials, malicious code or instructions intended to compromise users.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary. To report a conduct concern, use the contact route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public report.
